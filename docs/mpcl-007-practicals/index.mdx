# MPCL-007: Practicum in Experimental Psychology and Psychological Testing

## Course Overview

MPCL-007 is the hands-on practical component of the IGNOU MAPC programme, carrying **8 credits**. It bridges theoretical knowledge from the six theory courses with direct application — requiring learners to conduct standardised psychological experiments and administer psychometric tests under supervised conditions.

The practicum develops core professional competencies in:
- Administering and scoring psychological tests with standardised procedures
- Conducting controlled laboratory experiments
- Writing formal practicum reports in APA-compliant format
- Interpreting results in light of norms, theories, and introspective data
- Conducting viva voce (oral examination)

---

## The 8 Mandatory Practicals

| # | Practical | Type | Focus Area |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bhatia's Battery of Performance Test of Intelligence** | Psychological Test | Cognitive assessment |
| 2 | **16 PF (Sixteen Personality Factor Questionnaire)** | Psychological Test | Personality traits |
| 3 | **Vineland Social Maturity Scale** | Psychological Test | Adaptive behaviour |
| 4 | **Family Pathology Scale** | Psychological Test | Family dynamics |
| 5 | **Sociometry** | Assessment Technique | Interpersonal relationships |
| 6 | **Problem Behaviour Checklist** | Psychological Test | Conduct/emotional problems |
| 7 | **Span of Attention Experiment** | Experiment | Cognitive psychology |
| 8 | **Memory Experiment** | Experiment | Memory and learning |

Five practicals are written in full format in the practical notebook (internal assessment). All eight may be examined in the term-end practical examination.

## Practicum Report Format

Every practical written in the notebook must follow this standard format:

1. **Title** — Name of the test or experiment
2. **Aims/Objectives** — Purpose of the practical
3. **Hypothesis** — For experiments only; states the expected cause-effect relationship
4. **Introduction** — Historical background; theoretical concepts; key definitions
5. **Description** — Test author, number of items, factors/dimensions, reliability, validity, time limit
6. **Materials Required** — All physical materials needed
7. **Subject's Profile** — Demographic details of the participant
8. **Procedure and Administration** — Preparation, rapport building, standardised instructions, precautions
9. **Introspective Report** — Subject's first-person account of experience during the practical
10. **Scoring and Interpretation** — Scores computed using key; interpreted against norms
11. **Discussion** — Results discussed in light of theory and introspective report
12. **Conclusion** — Summary of findings
13. **References** — APA format citations
