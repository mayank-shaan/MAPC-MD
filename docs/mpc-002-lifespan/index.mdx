# MPC-002: Life Span Psychology

## Course Overview

This course examines human development across the entire lifespan, from prenatal development through old age, covering physical, cognitive, and psychosocial changes.

## Course Structure

### Block 1: Foundations of Life Span Development
- Concept of Development, Growth, and Life Span Perspective
- Prenatal Development
- Development During Infancy
- Early Childhood

### Block 2: Development During Early School Years
- Physical Development (6-11 Years)
- Cognitive, Social, Emotional, and Moral Development
- Schooling and Development
- Identification of Problems in School Children

### Block 3: Development During Adolescence
- Physical Changes
- Cognitive Development
- Identity, Self-Concept, Self-Esteem, Peer Groups
- Challenges and Issues in Adolescent Development

### Block 4: Adulthood and Ageing
- Physical Changes (Early, Middle, Old Age)
- Cognitive Changes (Early, Middle, Old Age)
- Psychosocial Changes (Early, Middle, Old Age)
- Challenges and Issues in Ageing

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:
- 🎯 Understand developmental processes across the lifespan
- 🎯 Identify developmental milestones and challenges
- 🎯 Apply developmental theories to real-world contexts
- 🎯 Recognize and address developmental issues

## Assessment Pattern

- **Theory**: 70 marks
- **Assignment**: 30 marks
- **Total**: 100 marks
