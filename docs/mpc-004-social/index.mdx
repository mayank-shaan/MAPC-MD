# MPC-004: Advanced Social Psychology

## Course Overview

This course examines advanced topics in social psychology, including social cognition, influence, attitudes, group dynamics, and interpersonal relationships.

## Course Structure

### Block 1: Introduction to Social Psychology
- Nature and Concept of Social Psychology
- Social Cognition and Attribution Theory
- Methods of Social Psychology
- Current Trends and Ethical Issues

### Block 2: Process of Social Influence
- Concept of Social Influence
- Pro-social Behaviour
- Interpersonal Attraction
- Aggression and Violence

### Block 3: Attitudes, Stereotypes, Prejudice and Discrimination
- Introduction to Attitude and Stereotypes
- Formation of Attitude and Attitude Change
- Prejudice and Discrimination
- Social Conflict and Its Resolution

### Block 4: Group Dynamics
- Introduction to Group, Formation and Types
- Group Dynamics
- Social Identity, Crowding and Crowd Behaviour

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:
- 🎯 Understand social psychological theories and research
- 🎯 Analyze social influence processes and group dynamics
- 🎯 Apply social psychology concepts to real-world issues
- 🎯 Evaluate attitudes, prejudice, and discrimination

## Assessment Pattern

- **Theory**: 70 marks
- **Assignment**: 30 marks
- **Total**: 100 marks
