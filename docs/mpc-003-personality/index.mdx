# MPC-003: Personality Theories and Assessment

## Course Overview

This course explores major personality theories and assessment methods, covering psychodynamic, trait, humanistic, and social-cognitive approaches.

## Course Structure

### Block 1: Introduction to Personality
- Definition and Concept of Personality
- Type and Trait Approaches
- Assessment of Personality
- Key Issues in Personality

### Block 2: Psychodynamic Theories
- Psychodynamic Theory (Freud, Horney, Sullivan)
- Social Cognitive Theory (Bandura)
- Learning Theory (Pavlov and Skinner)
- Humanistic and Self Theory (Maslow and Rogers)

### Block 3: Dispositional and Trait Theories
- Gordon Allport — Dispositional Theory
- Raymond Cattell — Trait Theory
- Hans Eysenck — Trait-Type Theory
- Big Five Factors

### Block 4: Introduction to Assessment and Testing
- Introduction to Assessment and Testing
- Self-Report Inventories
- Projective Techniques and Behavioural Assessment
- Other Measures of Personality

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:
- 🎯 Understand major personality theories and their applications
- 🎯 Compare and contrast different theoretical approaches
- 🎯 Apply personality assessment methods appropriately
- 🎯 Evaluate the strengths and limitations of various theories

## Assessment Pattern

- **Theory**: 70 marks
- **Assignment**: 30 marks
- **Total**: 100 marks
