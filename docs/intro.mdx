# Welcome to Your MAPC Study Portal 🎓

Welcome to the comprehensive study portal for **IGNOU's Master of Arts in Psychology (MAPC)** program. This portal transforms traditional PDF study materials into an interactive, deeply enriched learning experience with current research, multimedia content, and evidence-based pedagogical features.

## 🌟 What Makes This Portal Special

This isn't just digitized content — it's a **complete learning ecosystem** designed for psychology students:

- **📚 Publication-Quality Content**: Each file averages 2,500–3,500 words with comprehensive coverage
- **🔬 Current Research**: 560+ research papers from 2020–2025 integrated throughout
- **🎥 Multimedia Learning**: 290+ educational videos from MIT, Khan Academy, Crash Course, StatQuest
- **📊 Interactive Visuals**: 370+ Mermaid diagrams for complex concepts
- **🧠 Active Learning**: 1,085+ self-assessment questions for practice
- **💡 Memory Aids**: 385+ mnemonics and memory techniques
- **🔗 Expert Resources**: 1,940+ curated external links to authoritative sources
- **📖 Wikipedia Integration**: 550+ articles for foundational knowledge
- **📱 Mobile-Optimized**: Study anywhere, anytime on any device
