### 1. Sternberg's Information Processing Approach
**21.4% of papers** | 6/28 papers

Distinct from Sternberg's Triarchic Theory. This focuses on his componential analysis of intelligence in terms of information processing steps.

**Key sub-topics**: Metacomponents, performance components, knowledge-acquisition components, application to test-taking, comparison with general information processing models
