# 🔴 High Priority Topics — MPC-001

These 28 topics appear in **25% or more** of MPC-001 exam papers (2011–2024). Together they account for **90%+ of all exam questions**. Master these topics before moving to medium or low priority.

---

## Problem Solving (Block 4)

### 1. Problem Solving Strategies & Techniques
**96.4% of papers** | 27/28 papers | 29 questions total

The single most-tested topic in MPC-001. Every paper has at least one question on strategies, techniques, stages, or specific methods of problem solving.

**Key sub-topics**: Stages of problem solving, algorithms vs heuristics, specific techniques (brainstorming, analogy, means-ends analysis, working backward), trial and error

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/problem-solving-strategies) | [Block 4 Content →](/mpc-001/block-4/nature-stages-problem-solving)

---

### 2. Blocks to Problem Solving
**78.6% of papers** | 22/28 papers | 22 questions

The **most repeated question theme** with 13 appearances. Appears as both long questions (emotional, intellectual, cultural, environmental blocks) and short notes (functional fixedness, mental set, Einstellung).

**Key sub-topics**: Emotional blocks, intellectual blocks, cultural blocks, environmental blocks, perceptual blocks, functional fixedness, mental set (Einstellung effect), Luchins Water Jar

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/blocks-to-problem-solving) | [Block 4 Content →](/mpc-001/block-4/psychological-barriers-problem-solving)

---

### 3. Problem Solving — Definition & Types
**50% of papers** | 14/28 papers

Questions on the nature of problems, types of problems (well-defined vs ill-defined), and basic concepts in problem solving.

**Key sub-topics**: Nature of problems, well-defined vs ill-defined problems, problem space, operators, initial/goal states, insight problems

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/problem-solving-definition-types) | [Block 4 Content →](/mpc-001/block-4/types-characteristics-problems)

---

### 4. Problem Solving — Newell's Approach & AI
**46.4% of papers** | 13/28 papers

Consistently appears 1–2 times per paper as medium or long questions.

**Key sub-topics**: Newell & Simon's information processing approach, problem space hypothesis, General Problem Solver (GPS), AI in problem solving, heuristics in GPS

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/newell-approach-ai) | [Block 4 Content →](/mpc-001/block-4/newell-problem-space-hypothesis)

---

### 5. Problem Solving — Factors & Blocks
**28.6% of papers** | 8/28 papers

Overlaps with "Blocks" topic but focuses more on facilitating and hindering factors, individual differences in problem solving.

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/problem-solving-factors-blocks)

## Creativity (Block 2)

### 6. Creativity — Meaning, Aspects & Stages
**82.1% of papers** | 23/28 papers | 24 questions

Second-highest frequency topic. Appears in almost every paper, often as a Section A long question.

**Key sub-topics**: Meaning and definition of creativity, 4 Ps (Person, Process, Product, Press), stages of creativity (Wallas: Preparation, Incubation, Illumination, Verification), measurement of creativity (TTCT)

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/creativity-meaning-aspects-stages) | [Block 2 Content →](/mpc-001/block-2/creativity-meaning-aspects-theories)

---

### 7. Investment & Confluence Theory of Creativity
**28.6% of papers** | 8/28 papers | 8 questions — REPEATED 7 TIMES

One of the most reliably repeated long-answer topics. Sternberg & Lubart's Investment Theory is a favourite exam question.

**Key sub-topics**: Sternberg & Lubart's Investment Theory, "buy low sell high" in creativity, Confluence Model (Csikszentmihalyi), creative environment and resources

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/investment-confluence-creativity) | [Block 2 Content →](/mpc-001/block-2/investment-confluence-theory-creativity)

---

### 8. Creativity–Intelligence Relationship
**32.1% of papers** | 9/28 papers

**Key sub-topics**: Threshold theory, relationship between IQ and creativity, divergent vs convergent thinking, Guilford's contribution

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/creativity-intelligence-relationship) | [Block 2 Content →](/mpc-001/block-2/creativity-intelligence-relationship)

## Cognitive Psychology & Memory (Block 1)

### 9. Cognitive Psychology — History & Definition
**78.6% of papers** | 22/28 papers

Very frequently asked, either as a standalone question or combined with key issues.

**Key sub-topics**: Origins and history, Wundt's introspection, behaviourism era, cognitive revolution (Chomsky, Miller, Neisser), definition of cognitive psychology, domains of study

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/cognitive-psychology-history-definition) | [Block 1 Content →](/mpc-001/block-1/history-cognitive-psychology)

---

### 10. Information Processing
**71.4% of papers** | 20/28 papers

Core theory of cognitive psychology; asked in various forms — principles, models, learning and memory applications.

**Key sub-topics**: Information processing model (input-processing-output), limited capacity, serial vs parallel processing, Sternberg's information processing approach, application to learning and memory

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/information-processing) | [Block 1 Content →](/mpc-001/block-1/information-processing-model)

---

### 11. Key Issues in Cognitive Psychology
**42.9% of papers** | 12/28 papers

**Key sub-topics**: Nature vs nurture, modularity, connectionism vs symbolic AI, ecological validity, reductionism, domain specificity

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/key-issues-cognitive-psychology) | [Block 1 Content →](/mpc-001/block-1/key-issues-cognitive-psychology)

---

### 12. Research Methods in Cognitive Psychology
**50% of papers** | 14/28 papers

**Key sub-topics**: Experimental method, reaction time studies, cognitive neuropsychology, neuroimaging (fMRI, EEG, PET), case study method, think-aloud protocol, computational modeling

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/research-methods) | [Block 1 Content →](/mpc-001/block-1/research-methods-cognitive-psychology)

### 13. Memory Models
**57.1% of papers** | 16/28 papers — REPEATED 7 TIMES

Atkinson-Shiffrin Multi-Store Model and Waugh-Norman Primary-Secondary Memory model are both frequently asked.

**Key sub-topics**: Atkinson & Shiffrin Stage Model (sensory, STM, LTM), Waugh & Norman's Primary/Secondary memory model, comparison between models, evidence supporting each model

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/memory-models) | [Block 1 Content →](/mpc-001/block-1/atkinson-shiffrin-stage-model)

---

### 14. Memory Systems — STM, LTM, Sensory Memory
**57.1% of papers** | 16/28 papers — REPEATED 7 TIMES

Questions on the characteristics, types, and differences between memory systems.

**Key sub-topics**: Sensory memory (iconic, echoic), short-term memory (capacity — Miller's 7±2, duration — 15–30 sec), long-term memory (episodic, semantic, procedural), encoding differences

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/memory-systems) | [Block 1 Content →](/mpc-001/block-1/learning-memory-systems)

---

### 15. Neuropsychology of Learning & Memory
**60.7% of papers** | 17/28 papers — REPEATED 7+ TIMES

**Key sub-topics**: Role of hippocampus in memory consolidation, amygdala and emotional memory, cerebral cortex and long-term storage, amnesia types (anterograde/retrograde), case studies (H.M., Clive Wearing), cellular mechanisms (LTP)

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/neuropsychology-learning-memory) | [Block 1 Content →](/mpc-001/block-1/memory-brain-systems-amnesia)

---

### 16. Levels of Processing Model
**25% of papers** | 7/28 papers — REPEATED 6 TIMES

Reliably asked as a long question when it appears. Craik & Lockhart is a classic must-know model.

**Key sub-topics**: Shallow vs deep processing, phonological/semantic levels, elaborative rehearsal vs maintenance rehearsal, evidence (Hyde & Jenkins), self-reference effect, criticisms

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/levels-of-processing) | [Block 1 Content →](/mpc-001/block-1/levels-of-processing-craik-lockhart)

## Language (Block 3 & 4)

### 24. Language Acquisition
**60.7% of papers** | 17/28 papers — REPEATED 8–10 TIMES

One of the most reliably tested language topics. Both behaviouristic AND innateness theories are frequently asked together or separately.

**Key sub-topics**: Behaviouristic theory (Skinner — operant conditioning), Innateness/Nativist theory (Chomsky — LAD, UG, poverty of stimulus), stages of language acquisition (babbling, holophrastic, telegraphic), second language acquisition

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/language-acquisition) | [Block 3 Content →](/mpc-001/block-3/stages-language-acquisition)

---

### 25. Innateness Theory of Language Acquisition
**25% of papers** | 7/28 papers

Chomsky's theory is frequently asked on its own as a Section A long question.

**Key sub-topics**: Language Acquisition Device (LAD), Universal Grammar, critical period hypothesis, arguments against behaviourism, Chomsky vs Skinner

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/innateness-theory-language) | [Block 3 Content →](/mpc-001/block-3/language-acquisition-theories-behaviorist-innateness)

---

### 26. Multilingualism
**64.3% of papers** | 18/28 papers — REPEATED 13 TIMES

Among the top 3 most-tested topics. Consistently asked in both June and December.

**Key sub-topics**: Definition and types of multilingualism, coordinate vs compound vs subordinate bilinguals, additive vs subtractive bilingualism, single vs dual system hypothesis, multilingualism and thinking, cognitive benefits of multilingualism, code switching

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/multilingualism) | [Block 3 Content →](/mpc-001/block-3/multilingualism-basic-concepts)

---

### 27. Language Disorders & Aphasia
**50% of papers** | 14/28 papers — REPEATED 7+ TIMES

**Key sub-topics**: Types of aphasia (Broca's, Wernicke's, Anomic, Conduction, Global), Broca's area vs Wernicke's area, acquired vs developmental language disorders, autism and SLI

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/language-disorders-aphasia) | [Block 3 Content →](/mpc-001/block-3/language-disorders-aphasia-autism-learning-disabilities)

---

### 28. Speech Disorders

**Study**: [Questions & Answers →](/exam-prep/mpc-001/questions/speech-disorders) | [Block 3 Content →](/mpc-001/block-3/speech-disorders-apraxia-dysprosody-dysarthria)
