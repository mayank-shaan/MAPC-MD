## 🎯 Exam Strategy

### Paper Pattern (Current Format — 2015 onwards)

| Section | Questions | Marks Each | Total | Strategy |
|---------|-----------|------------|-------|----------|
| A | Answer 2 of 4 | 10 marks | 20 | Pick topics you know deeply — write ~500 words each |
| B | Answer 4 of 8 | 6 marks | 24 | Breadth matters — write ~300 words each |
| C | Answer 2 of 4 | 3 marks | 6 | Short notes — write ~150 words each |

### Time Management (2-hour exam)
- **Reading & planning**: 10 minutes
- **Section A** (2 × ~24 min): 48 minutes  
- **Section B** (4 × ~12 min): 48 minutes  
- **Section C** (2 × ~5 min): 10 minutes  
- **Review**: 4 minutes  

### Question Selection Strategy
1. Scan all questions first before writing anything
2. For Section A — pick topics where you can give definitions, theories, research evidence, and a diagram
3. For Section B — spread across different blocks/topics to show breadth
4. For Section C — choose topics where you know a crisp definition + 2–3 key points
5. Prioritize questions from the **Top 10 Topics** list below
