## 📖 Quick Topic Summaries

### 1. Prejudice and Discrimination
The single most-tested topic. Questions cover: methods of reducing prejudice, stereotypes (development and maintenance), manifestation of prejudice, causal factors. Reducing prejudice appears in 14 of 28 papers.

**What to study**: Contact hypothesis, social identity theory, categorisation, media role, legislation, education, stereotypes as cognitive shortcuts.

### 2. Group Dynamics
Joint most-tested topic. Questions cover: definition and characteristics of groups, types of groups, rules/norms, stages of development, cohesion, communication.

**What to study**: Tuckman's stages, social loafing, groupthink, reference groups vs membership groups, formal vs informal groups.

### 3. Attitudes
Third most-tested topic. Questions cover: nature and components (ABC), attitude formation factors, attitude change, theories of attitude organisation (Katz, Kelman).

**What to study**: Classical conditioning in attitude formation, cognitive dissonance, elaboration likelihood model, Heider's balance theory.

### 4. Conformity and Obedience
Appears in almost every paper. Questions cover: Asch's line experiment, factors increasing conformity, Milgram's obedience study, compliance (Cialdini's principles).

**What to study**: Asch study methodology and findings, Milgram's procedures and ethical controversy, informational vs normative influence, foot-in-door technique.

### 5. Research Methods
Appears every year. Questions cover: experimental method, ethical issues, ethnography, correlation method, observational methods, threats to validity.

**What to study**: APA ethical principles, demand characteristics, ecological validity, quasi-experimental design, meta-analysis.

### 6. Person Perception and Attribution
Questions cover: attribution errors (FAE, self-serving bias), Kelley's covariation model, Jones-Davis correspondent inference, schemas, primacy-recency effect.

**What to study**: Fundamental attribution error, actor-observer bias, Kelley's cube model, cognitive algebra, impression formation.

### 7. Aggression
Questions cover: frustration-aggression hypothesis, psychodynamic theory, social learning theory, types of aggression, methods to reduce aggression.

**What to study**: Berkowitz's modification, Bandura's Bobo doll study, catharsis debate, situational cues, deindividuation.

### 8. Social Conflict
Questions cover: nature and forms of conflict, methods of conflict resolution, Blake-Mouton two-dimensional model, causes and characteristics.

**What to study**: Types of conflict (intrapersonal, interpersonal, intergroup), negotiation, mediation, arbitration, BATNA, five conflict styles.

### 9. Interpersonal Attraction
Questions cover: factors affecting attraction (proximity, similarity, physical attractiveness, reciprocity), evolutionary theory, Heider's balance theory.

**What to study**: Mere exposure effect, matching hypothesis, propinquity effect, Sternberg's triangular theory of love.

### 10. Pro-social Behaviour
Questions cover: definitions, theories (empathy-altruism, negative-state relief, social exchange), Latané and Darley's 5-step model, factors affecting helping.

**What to study**: Bystander effect, diffusion of responsibility, kin selection, norm of social responsibility, reciprocity norm.

### 11. Collective and Crowd Behaviour
Questions cover: crowd behaviour theories (Le Bon, Allport, Emergent Norm Theory), collective behaviour forms, deindividuation, contagion.

**What to study**: Le Bon's crowd mind, Turner-Killian emergent norm theory, social comparison and crowd behaviour, rumour spread.
