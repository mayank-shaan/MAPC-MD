## 📝 Master Answer — Section B (250 words / 6 marks)

### Q. Explain the purpose and types of case study method. (6 marks)

*Also: "Critically analyze the case study method." (2014-Jun); "Discuss case study method as a study of individual behaviour." (2020-Jun)*

**Case Study Method**

The case study method is an in-depth, comprehensive investigation of a single individual, group, or event. It is an idiographic approach — seeking to understand the particular rather than establish general laws. In personality assessment, it involves collecting data from multiple sources (interviews, psychological tests, personal documents, medical records, observations) to construct a detailed understanding of the individual's personality and behaviour.

**Purpose**

1. *Clinical purpose*: Formulate diagnosis, plan treatment, monitor progress
2. *Research purpose*: Generate hypotheses, illustrate theoretical concepts, study rare conditions
3. *Educational purpose*: Training therapists through case examples

**Types of Case Studies**

1. **Illustrative Case Study**: Describes a particular situation or individual to illustrate a concept or theory (e.g., Freud's case studies — Little Hans, Dora)
2. **Exploratory Case Study**: Preliminary investigation into a poorly understood phenomenon to generate hypotheses for further research
3. **Critical Instance Case Study**: Examines a single case that challenges or confirms a theoretical assumption
4. **Clinical/In-depth Case Study**: Comprehensive study for diagnosis and treatment planning — the most common type in clinical practice

**Strengths**

- Rich, holistic data; captures complexity of individual functioning
- Useful when experimental methods are impractical or unethical
- Generates hypotheses for quantitative research

**Limitations**

- Cannot generalise findings to other individuals
- Prone to researcher bias and selective interpretation
- No control over variables; cannot establish causal relationships
- Time-consuming and resource-intensive

## 💡 Exam Tips

- Always list **4 types** with examples — this is the most frequently tested component.
- "Critically analyze" questions require balanced coverage of both strengths and limitations.
- Freud's case studies (Little Hans, Dora, Schreber) are classic examples — mentioning them earns marks.
