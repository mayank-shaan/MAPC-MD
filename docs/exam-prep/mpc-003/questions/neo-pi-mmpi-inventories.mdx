## 📝 Master Answer — Section B (250 words / 6 marks)

### Q. Explain the Neo-Personality Inventory (NEO-PI). (6 marks)

*Also: "MMPI-2" (2024-Jun C10, 2020-Jun C11, 2019-Dec C10); "Discuss in detail NEO-Personality Inventory." (2017-Jun B9)*

**NEO Personality Inventory (NEO-PI-R)**

The **NEO Personality Inventory-Revised (NEO-PI-R)** was developed by Paul Costa and Robert McCrae (1992) to operationalise the Five Factor Model of personality.

**Structure**: 240 items rated on a 5-point Likert scale. Measures all five factors (OCEAN) and **30 facets** (6 per factor). For example, Neuroticism facets include: Anxiety, Angry Hostility, Depression, Self-Consciousness, Impulsiveness, Vulnerability.

**Forms**: Form S (self-report) and Form R (observer rating by spouse/colleague) — their agreement provides convergent evidence for validity.

**Strengths**: Comprehensive coverage of normal personality; strong cross-cultural validity (replicated in 50+ countries); good reliability (α > 0.85 for factors); useful in clinical, research, and occupational settings.

**Abbreviated version**: NEO-FFI (60 items, 5 factor scores only) for quick screening.

---

**MMPI-2 (Minnesota Multiphasic Personality Inventory-2)**

Developed by Hathaway and McKinley (1943), revised in 1989 as **MMPI-2**.

**Structure**: 567 true/false items. 10 clinical scales: Hypochondriasis (Hs), Depression (D), Hysteria (Hy), Psychopathic Deviance (Pd), Masculinity-Femininity (Mf), Paranoia (Pa), Psychasthenia (Pt), Schizophrenia (Sc), Hypomania (Ma), Social Introversion (Si).

**Validity scales**: L (Lie), F (Frequency/Infrequency), K (Defensiveness) — detect faking and random responding.

**Uses**: Clinical diagnosis, forensic evaluation, personnel selection, treatment planning.

**Limitation**: Length (567 items); requires 6th-grade reading level; norms may not apply cross-culturally.
