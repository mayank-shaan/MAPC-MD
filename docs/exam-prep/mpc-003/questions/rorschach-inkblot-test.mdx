## 📝 Master Answer — Section B (250 words / 6 marks)

### Q. Describe Rorschach's test of personality and discuss its strengths and weaknesses. (6 marks)

*Also: "Explain ink-blot techniques of personality assessment." (2024-Jun B7); "Elucidate the administration and scoring of Rorschach test." (2024-Dec B8); "Explain the types of inkblot tests." (2022-Dec B9)*

**Rorschach Ink-Blot Test**

The Rorschach test, developed by Swiss psychiatrist Hermann Rorschach (1921), is the most widely used projective personality assessment instrument. It consists of **10 standardised inkblot cards**: 5 achromatic (black/grey/white), 2 partially coloured (black + red), and 3 fully coloured.

**Administration**

Two phases:
1. *Free Association Phase*: Cards are presented one at a time. The examiner asks "What might this be?" Responses are recorded verbatim along with card position and latency.
2. *Inquiry Phase*: After all 10 cards, the examiner revisits responses to clarify location (where on the card), determinants (what made it look that way — form, colour, movement, shading), and content (what was seen).

**Scoring (Exner's Comprehensive System)**

Responses are scored along four dimensions:
- **Location**: Where on the card (whole card W, common detail D, unusual detail Dd)
- **Determinants**: Form (F), chromatic colour (C/FC/CF), achromatic colour (C'), movement (M, FM, m), shading (T, V, Y)
- **Content**: Human (H), animal (A), anatomy (An), blood (Bl), etc.
- **Popularity**: Whether the response is commonly given (P) or unusual (O)

The **Structural Summary** integrates scores into indices assessing: emotional functioning, cognitive style, interpersonal perception, self-perception, and stress tolerance.

**Strengths**
- Rich clinical data; difficult to fake
- Useful for detecting psychopathology and unconscious processes
- Exner's system improved reliability significantly

**Weaknesses**
- Low reliability without standardised scoring
- Validity remains contested (Wood et al., 2003)
- Cultural norms may not apply universally
- Requires extensive training for valid interpretation
