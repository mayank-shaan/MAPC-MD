## 📝 Answer — Section C (100 words / 3 marks)

### Q. Differences between Introverts and Extroverts. (short note, 3 marks)

*Asked in: 2024-Jun C12, 2022-Jun C12, 2019-Jun C12*

**Introvert vs Extrovert (Eysenck)**

| Dimension | Introvert | Extravert |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Social behaviour | Reserved, prefers solitude | Sociable, seeks company |
| Arousal | High baseline cortical arousal | Low baseline cortical arousal |
| Stimulation preference | Avoids intense stimulation | Seeks intense stimulation |
| Conditionability | Conditions more easily | Conditions less easily |
| Occupational style | Works well alone; reflective | Works well with others; action-oriented |
| Risk-taking | Cautious | Sensation-seeking |

**Neurophysiological basis**: Introverts have higher ARAS-mediated cortical arousal and consequently seek quieter environments. Extraverts have lower cortical arousal and seek external stimulation to reach their optimal arousal level.
