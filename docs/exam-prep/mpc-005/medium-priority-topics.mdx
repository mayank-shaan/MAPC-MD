## 📖 Topic Summaries

### 1. Causal-Comparative Design
**Most likely as**: Section B (6-mark). Questions compare it to correlational design or experimental design. Both lack random assignment but differ in whether the independent variable is a pre-existing grouping factor (causal-comparative) or measured (correlational).

### 2. Hypothesis
**Most likely as**: Section B (6-mark). Questions cover: types (null, directional, non-directional), sources of hypotheses, difficulties in formulation, Type I and Type II errors.

### 3. Field Experiment
**Most likely as**: Section A or B. Key issue: greater ecological validity than laboratory experiments but lower internal validity due to less control. Steps in conducting a field experiment.

### 4. Research Design (general)
**Most likely as**: Section A or B. Definition of research design, its objectives (maximise validity, minimise error), and the general criteria of a good design.

### 5. Content Analysis
**Most likely as**: Section C (short note). Define content analysis, distinguish from discourse analysis, mention manifest vs latent content, coding categories, and reliability of coding.

### 6. Research Biases
**Most likely as**: Section C (short note) or Section B. Types of bias: experimenter bias, demand characteristics, response bias, sampling bias. Strategies to minimise: double-blind design, standardised procedures, random sampling.
