## 🎯 Exam Pattern

The exam format changed from 2015. All recent papers (2015–2024) follow this structure:

| Section | Questions Given | Answer | Marks Each | Word Limit | Total |
|---------|----------------|--------|------------|------------|-------|
| **Section A** (Long/Computation) | 4 | Any 2 | 10 marks | ~450 words or full computation | 20 |
| **Section B** (Medium) | 5 | Any 4 | 6 marks | ~250 words or computation | 24 |
| **Section C** (Short Notes) | 3 | Any 2 | 3 marks | ~100 words | 6 |
| | | | | **Total** | **50** |

> **Note (Pre-2015 Format):** Papers from 2011–2014 had 10 questions, answer any 5 — each 10 marks. Calculator is permitted throughout.

### Time Management
- **Total time**: 2 hours
- **Section A**: ~25 minutes per answer (2 answers = 50 min; include full computation steps)
- **Section B**: ~12 minutes per answer (4 answers = 48 min)
- **Section C**: ~6 minutes per note (2 notes = 12 min)
- **Reading & selection**: 10 minutes

### Question Selection Strategy
1. Section A is almost always one conceptual + one computation question; choose accordingly
2. ANOVA computation and Chi-square computation appear virtually every paper — prepare both fully
3. Parametric vs non-parametric is the safest conceptual Section A choice
4. Section B fixtures: Mann-Whitney U computation + Two-way ANOVA / interactional effect
5. Section C short notes rotate through: Type I/II errors, Level of significance, Degrees of freedom, Kurtosis
