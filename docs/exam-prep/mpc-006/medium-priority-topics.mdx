- **Level of Significance**: Often appears as part of Hypothesis Testing answers; also comes as standalone Section C short note. Prepare the 5–6 steps in setting significance levels.
- **Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test**: Non-parametric alternative to paired t-test. Prepare the step-by-step ranked difference procedure.
- **Multiple Regression**: Usually a short conceptual question — distinguish from simple regression and explain multiple R.
- **Linear & Non-linear Relationship**: Often paired with a Spearman's Rho computation — explain both types with diagrams.
- **Point & Interval Estimation**: Straightforward conceptual distinction; appears as Section C short note.
- **Skewness & Kurtosis**: Overlaps with Divergence from Normality; prepare diagrams of positive/negative skewness and leptokurtic/platykurtic/mesokurtic.
