## 🎯 Must-Know Concepts
1. Non-parametric alternative to paired samples t-test
2. Based on ranks of absolute differences between paired scores
3. Both magnitude and direction of differences are considered
4. T statistic = smaller of sum of positive and negative ranks
5. Decision: T obtained ≤ T critical = significant

### Answer (Section B — ~250 words):

The **Wilcoxon Matched Pair Signed Rank Test** is a non-parametric statistical test used to compare two **related (paired) samples**. It is the non-parametric equivalent of the paired samples t-test.

**When to Use:**
1. Data consists of paired observations (before-after, matched pairs, repeated measures)
2. Data is at least ordinal
3. Assumptions of the paired t-test (normality of difference scores) cannot be met
4. Sample is small

**Step-by-Step Procedure:**

**Step 1**: Compute the difference (D) for each pair: D = Score₂ − Score₁

**Step 2**: Exclude all pairs where D = 0 (zero differences are dropped). Update n accordingly.

**Step 3**: Rank the **absolute values** of the differences |D|, from smallest (rank 1) to largest. Handle ties with average ranks.

**Step 4**: Assign the **sign** of the original difference to each rank.
- Positive differences → positive ranks
- Negative differences → negative ranks

**Step 5**: Compute:
- W⁺ = sum of positive ranks
- W⁻ = sum of negative ranks

**Verification**: W⁺ + W⁻ = n(n+1)/2

**Step 6**: T = smaller of W⁺ and W⁻

**Step 7**: Find T critical from the Wilcoxon T table at the adjusted n and α = 0.05

**Decision**: If T obtained ≤ T critical → Reject H₀ → Significant difference between the two conditions

> **Note**: Like Mann-Whitney U, the decision rule is reversed — smaller obtained value means more significant.

**Advantages:**
- Considers both magnitude and direction of differences (unlike sign test)
- More powerful than the sign test
- Suitable when t-test assumptions cannot be met

## 🧠 Memory Aid

**Wilcoxon Steps (DRZST):**
- **D**ifferences (compute D = Score₂ − Score₁)
- **R**emove zeros
- **Z**ero-free ranking of |D|
- **S**ign the ranks (+ or −)
- **T** = smaller of W⁺ and W⁻ → compare with T critical
