## 📝 Master Answer (250 words / 6 marks)

### Q. What is ADHD? Discuss the remedial measures for ADHD programmes at school and in families. (6 marks)

**Definition**

**ADHD (Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder)** is a neurodevelopmental disorder characterised by persistent patterns of inattention, hyperactivity, and/or impulsivity that interfere with functioning and development. It is one of the most common childhood disorders, affecting approximately 5–7% of children, and often persists into adolescence and adulthood.

**Types of ADHD (DSM-5)**
1. **Predominantly Inattentive**: Difficulty sustaining attention, following instructions, organising tasks; easily distracted; forgetful
2. **Predominantly Hyperactive-Impulsive**: Excessive motor activity, talking, fidgeting; difficulty waiting; impulsive decision-making
3. **Combined Presentation**: Both inattentive and hyperactive-impulsive symptoms

**Key Symptoms**
- Cannot sustain attention on tasks or play activities
- Does not follow through on instructions
- Loses materials needed for tasks
- Blurts out answers; cannot wait for turn
- Runs or climbs excessively in inappropriate situations
- Seems not to listen when spoken to directly

**Identification**

Assessment includes: teacher and parent behaviour rating scales (e.g., Conners' Scale), structured clinical interviews, continuous performance tests (CPT), and educational achievement testing. Diagnosis requires symptoms in multiple settings (home and school) for at least 6 months.

**Remedial Measures**

*At School:*
- Preferential seating (front, away from distractions)
- Short, clear instructions; break tasks into steps
- Frequent breaks and movement opportunities
- Extended time on tests
- Token economy and positive reinforcement systems
- Collaborative planning with special educators

*At Home:*
- Consistent daily routines and structure
- Clear, short rules with immediate consequences
- Parent training programmes (e.g., Barkley's Parent Training)
- Homework support: quiet environment, scheduled breaks
- Coordination with school on behavioural strategies
- Family therapy to address relational stress

*Medical Management*: Stimulant medication (methylphenidate / Ritalin) combined with behavioural intervention is considered most effective.

## 💡 Exam Tips
- Always define ADHD + list the **three types** (inattentive, hyperactive-impulsive, combined)
- For remedial measures: **separate school strategies from home/family strategies** using sub-headings
- Mention **parent training** as an evidence-based family intervention
- Note that **combined medication + behavioural** intervention is most effective
