## 💡 Exam Strategy for Medium Topics

- **Language Development**, **Motor Development**, and **Cognitive Development in Adulthood** frequently appear in Section B
- **Self-Concept & Self-Esteem**, **Intellectual Disability**, and **School Skills** appear often as Section C short notes
- **Theories of Lifespan Development** (stage vs contextual approach) sometimes appears in Section A
- **Research Methods** often appears as an obstacle-based short note or medium question

**Recommended**: Prepare 250-word Section B answers for all 14 topics. For topics 9–14, also prepare 100-word short notes.
