## 🎯 Exam Pattern (Current Format — 2015 Onwards)

The exam format changed from 2015. All recent papers follow this structure:

| Section | Questions Given | Answer | Marks Each | Word Limit | Total |
|---------|----------------|--------|------------|------------|-------|
| **Section A** (Long) | 4 | Any 2 | 10 marks | ~450 words | 20 |
| **Section B** (Medium) | 5 | Any 4 | 6 marks | ~250 words | 24 |
| **Section C** (Short Notes) | 3 | Any 2 | 3 marks | ~100 words | 6 |
| | | | | **Total** | **50** |

> **Note (Pre-2015 Format):** Papers from 2011–2014 had 10 questions, answer any 5 — each 10 marks. Questions from those years are still valuable for topic probability analysis.

### Time Management
- **Total time**: 2 hours
- **Section A**: ~25 minutes per answer (2 answers = 50 min)
- **Section B**: ~12 minutes per answer (4 answers = 48 min)
- **Section C**: ~6 minutes per note (2 notes = 12 min)
- **Reading & selection**: 10 minutes

### Question Selection Strategy
1. Read all questions in each section before answering
2. In Section A, pick topics you can write 450 words on comfortably
3. In Section B, choose 4 from 5 — skip your weakest topic
4. In Section C, always prepare all identity/adolescence + late adulthood short notes — they recur constantly
5. Sub-part questions (e.g., 4+6) — plan your word count for each part separately
