# MPC-005: Research Methods in Psychology

## Course Overview

This course provides comprehensive training in psychological research methods, covering both quantitative and qualitative approaches, research design, and data analysis.

## Course Structure

### Block 1: Basic Concepts in Research
- Basic Process/Concept in Research
- Reliability and Validity
- Variables and Constructs
- Hypothesis Formulation and Sampling

### Block 2: Types of Research
- Survey Research
- Ex-Post Facto Research
- Experimental Research (Field Experiment)
- Case Study

### Block 3: Research Design
- Single Factor Design
- Factorial Design
- Quasi-Experimental Design
- Correlational and Causal Comparative Design

### Block 4: Qualitative Research in Psychology
- Qualitative Research and Ethnography
- Grounded Theory
- Discourse Analysis & Content Analysis
- Reporting and Evaluating in Qualitative Research

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:
- 🎯 Design and conduct psychological research studies
- 🎯 Select appropriate research methods and designs
- 🎯 Analyze and interpret research data
- 🎯 Evaluate research quality and ethical considerations

## Assessment Pattern

- **Theory**: 70 marks
- **Assignment**: 30 marks
- **Total**: 100 marks
